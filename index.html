Bong Jun Cha 차봉준

Creative Director, INNOCEAN · Founding lead of Creative X (now CX Creative)

I make brands something people can feel.

I spent two decades making advertising before becoming the first INNOCEAN creative director to extend my practice into spatial customer experience. After leading campaigns such as The Cheering Bandage and The Biggest Lifeguard, I moved beyond the screen to the places where people actually stand. In February 2025 INNOCEAN established Creative X, its first in-house creative team dedicated to spatial customer experience, and appointed me its founding Creative Director; in November 2026 the team was expanded into CX Creative, which I now lead. The team works as a creative judgment layer rather than a production unit, bringing production-grade craft into exhibitions, showrooms and brand spaces — designing not only what people see, but what they feel and go on to say.

20년 동안 광고를 만들었다. 치어링 밴디지와 비기스트 라이프가드로 광고에서 할 수 있는 것을 한 뒤, 이노션 제작 CD 가운데 처음으로 광고 바깥, 사람이 실제로 서 있는 공간으로 영역을 넓혔다. 2025년 2월 회사는 그 일을 전담하는 최초의 조직 Creative X를 만들어 초대 팀장을 맡겼고, 2026년 11월 CX Creative로 확대됐다. 광고가 아니라 사람이 남기고 싶어지는 경험을 만든다.
